# Project Gamma information retention handbook

Audit logs are retained for 90 calendar days.

Billing records are retained for 7 years.

Access logs ("log akses") are retained for 30 calendar days.

After identity is verified, a data deletion request is completed within 15 business days.

An active legal hold suspends deletion of the records covered by that hold.

An export link expires 14 calendar days after it is issued.
